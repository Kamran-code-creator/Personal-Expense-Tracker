# Personal-Expense-Tracker

# 1. Problem:

My father is a government employee, so often he struggles to manage his expanses like when my father's monthly salary arrives, he spends on things that doesn't matter at all.

I mean like I feel my father spends a good amount of his salary on things that he should save for the rest days of a month.

When my father's salary arrives, it disappears in just two to three days and my father struggles to find money for the rest days of a month.

Then I realized that my father needs to manage his expenses well and he shouldn't spend too much on things that aren't necessary or simply cut out the unnecessary expenses.

From there I found that idea like why I shouldn't help my father build him a PERSONAL EXPENSE TRACKER,

It can help my father manage his expenses well.

# 2. Solution

I built a PERSONAL EXPENSE TRACKER to help my father and other employees to manage there expenses like where they spend, why they spend and how they spend and stuff like that.

This product help users track their daily, weekly, monthly or even yearly expenses so that they can manage their things well and get themselves a comfortable living standards.

# 3. Technologies:

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

# 4. Target Audience

This product is specifically for employees who are working in government or private sector but other individuals can also use it if it works for them.

# 5. Final Note

This can be a good product for you, if you are struggling with managing your expenses.

It can help you limit your unnecessary spendings and potentially can help you save more money so that you can buy some great products of services for it.
